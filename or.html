Module 1: Linear Programming Problem (LPP)

Q1. Milk Plant Formulation
Question: Products A & B. Profit: Rs 5 (A), Rs 3 (B). Processing time: A needs 1 min on G, 2 min on H. B needs 1 min on G, 1 min on H. Machine G is available for 6 hrs 40 mins, Machine H for 8 hrs 20 mins. Formulate LPP to maximize profit.

Step 1: Identify Decision Variables

Humein nahi pata Product A aur B kitne banenge. Isliye A ko x₁ aur B ko x₂ maanenge.

Let x₁ = Number of units of Product A.
Let x₂ = Number of units of Product B.

Step 2: Formulate Objective Function

Humein Profit maximize (zyada se zyada) karna hai.

Maximize Z = 5x₁ + 3x₂

Step 3: Convert Time & Formulate Constraints

Hours aur minutes ko sirf minutes me convert karna padega kyunki processing time minutes me hai.

  • Machine G: 6 hours 40 mins = (6 × 60) + 40 = 400 minutes.
  • Machine H: 8 hours 20 mins = (8 × 60) + 20 = 500 minutes.

Machine G Constraint: 1x₁ + 1x₂ ≤ 400
Machine H Constraint: 2x₁ + 1x₂ ≤ 500

Step 4: Non-negativity Restriction

Production kabhi negative (-) nahi ho sakti.

x₁, x₂ ≥ 0

Q4. Graphical Method (Maximization)
Question: Maximize Z = 8x + y. Subject to: x + y ≤ 40, 2x + y ≤ 60, x, y ≥ 0.

Step 1: Find Coordinates for the Graph

Dono equations me ek baar x=0 aur dusri baar y=0 daal kar coordinates nikalenge.

Line 1 (x + y = 40):
If x = 0, y = 40 → (0, 40)
If y = 0, x = 40 → (40, 0)

Line 2 (2x + y = 60):
If x = 0, y = 60 → (0, 60)
If y = 0, x = 30 → (30, 0)

Step 2: Find Intersection Point

Jahan dono line graph me takrayengi, wo point nikalne ke liye dono equations ko subtract karenge.

(2x + y) - (x + y) = 60 - 40
x = 20
Substitute x = 20 in Line 1: 20 + y = 40 ⇒ y = 20
Intersection Point = (20, 20)

Step 3: Evaluate Corner Points in Z

Ab graph ka bounded area dekh kar sabhi corner points ki value "Z = 8x + y" me daal kar check karenge ki Maximize kaha ho raha hai.

Corner Points (x, y)Value of Z = 8x + y
(0, 0)0
(0, 40)40
(20, 20)8(20) + 20 = 180
(30, 0)8(30) + 0 = 240 (Maximum)

Final Answer: Maximum value of Z is 240 at x=30, y=0.

Q6. Formulate the Dual
Question: Formulate dual for Minimize Z = 6x + 5y. Subject to: x + y ≥ 8, 2x + 3y ≥ 18, 3x + y ≥ 15, x, y ≥ 0.

Step 1: Rules for Converting Primal to Dual

  • Minimization ka problem "Maximization" me badal jata hai.
  • Jo RHS (Right Hand Side) values (8, 18, 15) thi, wo nayi Objective equation ki values ban jayengi.
  • Jo purani Objective values (6, 5) thi, wo nayi Constraints ka RHS ban jayengi.
  • "≥" wale sign "≤" me badal jayenge.

Step 2: Final Dual Formulation

Let the new dual variables be w₁, w₂, w₃.

Maximize W = 8w₁ + 18w₂ + 15w₃

Subject to:
1w₁ + 2w₂ + 3w₃ ≤ 6
1w₁ + 3w₂ + 1w₃ ≤ 5
w₁, w₂, w₃ ≥ 0

Module 2: Transportation Problem (Step-by-Step)

Q1. North-West Corner (NWC) Method
Question: Solve using NWC Method. Find initial cost & check degeneracy.
Supply (20, 30, 25), Demand (10, 35, 30). Total = 75 (Balanced).

Step 1: Allocate from North-West (Top-Left Corner)

NWC method me hamesha sabse pehle cell (top-left) se start karte hain. Jiski capacity jaldi khatam hogi (Demand ya Supply), usko pura karke row ya column cross kar denge aur next me move karenge.

  • Start at (S1, D1): Supply 20, Demand 10. Allocate 10. D1 satisfied. Remaining Supply S1 = 10. Move Right.
  • Move to (S1, D2): Remaining Supply 10, Demand 35. Allocate 10. S1 exhausted. Remaining D2 = 25. Move Down.
  • Move to (S2, D2): Supply 30, Remaining Demand 25. Allocate 25. D2 satisfied. Remaining S2 = 5. Move Right.
  • Move to (S2, D3): Remaining Supply 5, Demand 30. Allocate 5. S2 exhausted. Remaining D3 = 25. Move Down.
  • Move to (S3, D3): Remaining Supply 25, Remaining Demand 25. Allocate 25. Both exhausted!

S₁10 (C:4)10 (C:6)(C:8)20
S₂(C:3)25 (C:5)5 (C:7)30
S₃(C:4)(C:6)25 (C:5)25
Demand10353075

Step 2: Calculate Cost & Degeneracy Check

Total cost nikalne ke liye jitna allocate kiya hai usko cost se multiply karenge. Aur Degeneracy check karne ke liye dekhenge ki allocated cells (m+n-1) ke barabar hain ya nahi.

Total Cost = (10×4) + (10×6) + (25×5) + (5×7) + (25×5) = 40 + 60 + 125 + 35 + 125 = 385

Degeneracy: Number of Allocations = 5. Formula: m + n - 1 = 3 rows + 3 cols - 1 = 5.
Since 5 = 5, the solution is Non-Degenerate.

Q2. Least Cost Method (LCM)
Question: Solve using Least Cost Method. Supply(20,30,40), Demand(30,20,40).

Step 1: Select the Lowest Cost

LCM me pure matrix me jo sabse choti cost hoti hai, waha sabse pehle allocation karte hain.

  • Lowest cost is 5 at (S3, D2). Allocate min(40, 20) = 20. D2 fulfilled. Cross Column D2.
  • Next lowest is 8 at (S1, D1) and (S3, D3). Pick (S1, D1). Allocate min(20, 30) = 20. S1 fulfilled. Cross Row S1.
  • Next lowest is 8 at (S3, D3). Allocate min(remaining S3:20, D3:40) = 20. Row S3 fulfilled. Cross Row S3.
  • Remaining row is S2. Allocate remaining demands to S2: (S2,D1)=10, (S2,D3)=20.

Step 2: Calculate Total Cost

Total Cost = (20×8) + (10×12) + (20×9) + (20×5) + (20×8) = 160 + 120 + 180 + 100 + 160 = 720

Q3. Vogel's Approximation Method (VAM)
Question: Solve using VAM. Supply(25,35,30), Demand(20,30,40).

Step 1: Calculate Penalties

VAM me har row aur column ki sabse 2 choti values ko minus karke Penalty likhte hain. Jis row/col ki penalty sabse badi hogi, usme sabse choti cost wale box me allocate karenge.

  • P1: Row penalties: R1(4), R2(2), R3(1). Col penalties: C1(1), C2(3), C3(1). Max penalty is 4 in Row 1. Min cost in R1 is 3. Allocate min(25,30)=25 in (S1,D2). S1 exhausted.
  • P2: Ignore Row 1. Row penalties: R2(2), R3(1). Col penalties: C1(1), C2(1), C3(3). Max penalty is 3 in Col 3. Min cost in C3 is 6. Allocate min(30,40)=30 in (S3,D3). S3 exhausted.
  • Final: Only Row 2 left. Allocate remaining demands directly to S2: (S2,D1)=20, (S2,D2)=5, (S2,D3)=10.

S₂20 (C:4)5 (C:6)10 (C:9)35
S₃(C:5)(C:7)30 (C:6)30

Step 2: Total Cost

Total Cost = (25×3) + (20×4) + (5×6) + (10×9) + (30×6) = 75 + 80 + 30 + 90 + 180 = 455

Q7, Q8, Q9. Transportation Maximization Rule

General Rule for Maximization

Jab bhi Transport me Maximization (Profit) ka sum aaye, toh sabse pehle table me se Sabse badi value (Highest Number) dhoondho. Fir us highest number me se pure table ki saari values ko minus karke ek nayi "Loss Matrix" banao. Uske baad normally NWC, LCM ya VAM apply karo. Aakhiri me Total Profit nikalne ke liye purane (original) table ki profit values se multiply karna.

Total Profit = 875
